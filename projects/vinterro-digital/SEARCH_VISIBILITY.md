## Current observed strength
The current homepage explicitly describes Vinterro as a creative growth partner and lists branding, social media, SEO, advertising, Shopify, web, app and photo/video capabilities. This is useful topical coverage.

## Current observed gaps / opportunities
- Search result/site title should stay tightly aligned with the Vinterro Digital entity and priority commercial positioning; avoid vague titles such as “Universe”.
- Do not rely on one homepage to rank for every service. Build substantial service pages with process, deliverables, fit, platform expertise and real evidence.
- Add/strengthen case-study pages with verifiable problem → work → outcome structure.
- Establish consistent Organization/WebSite entity markup, social `sameAs`, contact/business information and service-area truth.
- Verify Google Business Profile eligibility/category/services and keep website data consistent with it.
- Build genuine third-party corroboration: client project credits, partner/vendor directories, interviews, portfolio profiles, press and local/industry listings.
- Maintain author/expert attribution on substantive educational content.
- Publish non-commodity content derived from actual client work, audits, experiments and platform expertise.
