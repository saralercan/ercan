# Ayvalık Vibes — Search / AI Discovery Visibility Map

## Entity role
Editorial/local discovery brand for Ayvalık, Cunda, Küçükköy, Sarımsaklı and nearby experiences. Keep its role editorial/media/community-led so it does not become a duplicate of GoAyvalık's utility/app guide role.

## Priority query clusters
- Ayvalık rehberi
- Cunda rehberi
- Ayvalık gezilecek yerler
- Ayvalık etkinlikleri
- Cunda etkinlikleri
- Ayvalık restoran / meyhane / kafe editorial guides
- Ayvalık sanat / seramik / atölye
- Ayvalık plajları
- Ayvalık kültür sanat
- Ayvalık yerel üreticiler

## Search strategy
- Original local reporting, interviews, photography, event coverage and first-hand guides are the main moat.
- Dates, venues, addresses and event status must be fresh and source-backed.
- Build clear author/editor identity and update timestamps for editorial content.
- Use Article/BlogPosting, Event, Breadcrumb and organization/local entity markup only where content is truthful and supported.
- Maintain strong internal linking between locations, events, stories and evergreen guides.
- Avoid mass-generated “best” lists without firsthand evidence; local recommendation pages should explain methodology and selection rationale.

## AI recommendation readiness
AI systems are more likely to cite useful pages that contain clear, current, firsthand information and identifiable entities. Prioritize factual density, original local insight, source links and stable canonical URLs over keyword repetition.
