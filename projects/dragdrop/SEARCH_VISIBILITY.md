Do not self-proclaim “best” as the main strategy. Build recommendation eligibility through designer breadth, curation quality, genuine customer reputation, product/feed quality, editorial expertise, third-party designer links/mentions and consistent entity/product data.

## Current observed strength
The current homepage already describes Drag&Drop as “Bağımsız tasarımın ortak vitrini” and states that designers, producers and original pieces are brought together in a curated shopping experience. This is a strong entity/category proposition and should remain visible/indexable.

## Current observed opportunities
- Strengthen the homepage/entity metadata around the canonical category: curated design marketplace/store, independent designers and original design objects—without awkward keyword stuffing.
- Ensure `WebSite` + Organization/merchant identity data is consistent with the visible brand and canonical domain.
- Ensure Product/ProductGroup, brand/designer, price, availability, images and variants remain consistent between Shopify output, structured data and Merchant Center feeds.
- Build richer designer profile pages: bio, location, discipline, materials, process, official links, products and editorial stories.
- Connect product pages to designer entities and designer pages back to relevant products/collections.
- Build non-thin editorial content: designer interviews, material guides, room/lighting guides, gift guides, curation rationale, maker/process stories and original design trend analysis.
- Avoid auto-generated taxonomy/index bloat and duplicate locale/filter URLs competing with canonical collections.
- Maintain clean locale/hreflang behavior; current multilingual surfaces must not leak Turkish labels into German/other locale pages.
- Strengthen authentic external corroboration by encouraging designers/makers to link their official DragDrop profile/product collections from their own sites/social profiles where appropriate.

## Ecommerce discovery stack
- Google Merchant Center feed where eligible
- Shopify-native product structured data
- `ProductGroup`/variant correctness where applicable
- shipping/returns merchant data
- Search Console + Merchant diagnostics
- image SEO and high-quality product imagery
- canonical collection/product URLs
- internal breadcrumbs
- designer/brand entity graph

## Structured data candidates
- `WebSite`
- suitable merchant `Organization`/`OnlineStore` identity semantics
- `Product` / `ProductGroup`
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `ProfilePage` + Person/Organization for genuine designer profiles where semantically appropriate
- `Article`/`BlogPosting` for editorial content
- `VideoObject` for original maker/product videos where supported

Never fabricate aggregate ratings, review counts, scarcity or awards in schema.

## Information architecture priorities
- `/pages/tasarimcilar` + indexable individual designer profiles
- high-value canonical collections built around real product taxonomy
- `/blogs/...` editorial hub with meaningful internal linking to designers/products
- about/curation methodology
- delivery/returns/contact/business identity pages

## Measurement benchmark
Separate branded, category, designer, product, recommendation and editorial query clusters. Measure Search Console, Merchant Center diagnostics, product impressions/clicks, AI/search referrals, designer profile discovery, qualified product traffic and conversion—not vanity visibility alone.
