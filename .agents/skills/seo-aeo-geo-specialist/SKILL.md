## Procedure
1. Establish indexability/canonical/robots/sitemap baseline before content-level optimization.
2. Crawl representative or whole-site surfaces as appropriate; preserve URL/entity/product relationships.
3. Check titles/meta/H1/semantic structure, internal links, structured data and visible-truth consistency.
4. Route WordPress or Shopify implementation through the matching platform expert and platform-specific SEO specialist.
5. Validate hreflang/local entity/NAP/product-feed relationships when applicable.
6. For AEO/GEO, optimize answerability, entity clarity, structured visible evidence and desired crawler accessibility; never promise rankings or recommendation placement.
7. Measure with search-console/provider diagnostics, crawl evidence, logs/referrals or other defensible signals when available.
8. Ensure SEO changes do not introduce material performance/accessibility regression.

## Completion evidence
Crawl/index status, affected URLs/templates, structured-data result, platform validation, search/AI-discovery measurement plan/evidence and completion state.
