## Seven-module operating model

1. **Niche & positioning**
   - Define audience, problem space, demand signals, creator/brand fit, monetization paths and differentiation.
   - Compare opportunities using evidence, ranges and uncertainty. Do not invent RPM/CPM, demand or competition values.
   - Avoid "high income + low competition" as an unsupported shortcut.

2. **Channel identity**
   - Produce name options, promise, audience profile, voice, content pillars, visual/thumbnail behavior and a defensible unique mechanism.
   - Reuse the project brand system when the channel belongs to an existing brand.

3. **90-day programming**
   - Build a coherent sequence, not a random list.
   - Include search-led, recommendation-led, authority-building, proof/case-study, conversion and experimental content where relevant.
   - Specify cadence, dependencies, production weight and the hypothesis for every episode.

4. **Research & script**
   - Research claims before writing.
   - Default structure: hook -> context/problem -> evidence/value -> developed ideas/steps -> proof/examples -> CTA -> bridge to next content.
   - The first 30 seconds must deliver the promised topic quickly; avoid fake urgency, fabricated proof and filler.
   - Keep spoken language natural and channel-specific.

5. **Packaging & discovery**
   - Generate multiple title/thumbnail concepts with distinct hypotheses, not cosmetic variations.
   - Preserve exact claims and brand/product truth.
   - Produce description, chapters when appropriate, links, source notes and relevant metadata.
   - Do not treat tags, keyword density or a fixed title formula as guaranteed ranking levers.
   - Thumbnail/text tests must optimize comprehension and curiosity without misleading clickbait.

6. **Monetization system**
   - Map monetization by audience intent and channel maturity.
   - Candidate paths: YouTube program revenue, sponsorship, affiliate, owned digital/physical product, service/consulting, membership/community, newsletter/lead capture.
   - For each path record offer, qualification, audience fit, unit economics assumptions, conversion path, disclosure/compliance needs and measurement.
   - Never promise a revenue outcome or imply eligibility before current YouTube requirements are verified.

7. **Growth & repurposing**
   - Convert validated long-form ideas into platform-native Shorts/Reels, X posts/threads, LinkedIn, email, blog/Pinterest only where those channels matter.
   - Do not blindly cross-post identical cuts/copy.
   - Feed performance back into the next topic, hook, format and packaging tests.

## Canonical episode brief

Every production-ready episode should include:
- episode_id / working title
- audience + problem/job
- channel pillar
- strategic role: search / browse-recommendation / authority / conversion / experiment
- primary promise
- evidence/research sources
- hook options
- outline + key beats
- proof/examples
- CTA + next-video bridge
- title variants with hypothesis
- thumbnail variants with hypothesis
- description / chapters / links / disclosure notes
- long-form -> short-form repurposing plan
- production notes + asset needs
- risk/policy/fact checks
- publish status
- post-publish metrics + learning

## 90-day planning contract

A 90-day plan is not complete unless it includes:
- channel objective and target audience
- 3-5 durable content pillars
- a sequenced backlog with at least 12 meaningful long-form ideas unless the user asks for another cadence
- publication cadence and production capacity
- launch/authority/monetization phases where relevant
- title/thumbnail hypotheses
- analytics checkpoints
- repurposing map
- monetization experiments
- clear assumptions and unknowns

## Analytics loop

Use the strongest available YouTube Studio evidence and timestamp it. Relevant signals may include:
- impressions and traffic source
- click-through rate interpreted with context, not in isolation
- average view duration / average percentage viewed
- first-30-second retention and key-drop moments
- watch time
- returning vs new viewers
- subscribers gained/lost by video
- end-screen / next-video continuation where available
- Shorts viewed-vs-swiped and retention where available
- qualified clicks, leads, sales or sponsor outcomes when business goals exist

Loop:
`publish -> collect evidence -> diagnose topic/hook/packaging/retention/conversion -> hypothesis -> next variant -> retest`.

Do not declare a winner from tiny samples or compare unlike traffic sources without caveats.

## Publishing and external-action guardrails

- Content generation ability is not authenticated YouTube access.
- Channel creation, uploads, scheduling, edits, comments or monetization settings require an actually connected/authorized provider surface.
- Preserve account/brand/channel boundaries and least privilege.
- Before a public publish or irreversible external change, follow the active tool/provider approval contract.
- Record resulting video/channel IDs or permalinks when an external action actually succeeds.
- Never claim a video was published, scheduled or monetized without external-state evidence.

## Current-fact policy

At execution time verify volatile facts from official YouTube/Google sources when material, including:
- Partner Program eligibility and monetization policies
- reused/repetitious/AI-generated content policies
- disclosure requirements
- upload/Shorts/thumbnail/metadata limits
- copyright/music rules
- advertiser-friendly guidelines
- API/auth/publishing behavior

Do not freeze changing platform thresholds or limits into this skill.
