# Founder Operations

This JIT pack adapts useful workflow patterns from `ognjengt/founder-skills` without duplicating Ercan OS stable sales, marketing, brand, product or research identities.

## Stable-owner mapping
- StrategicPlanning -> `@Orchestrator` + task-relevant business specialist
- GTM -> sales/marketing strategy owners + `@SocialStrategy` / ads specialists as applicable
- SOP -> `@Orchestrator` + process owner
- PRD -> `@Orchestrator + @WebArchitecture/@MobileArchitect` and implementation owners as relevant
- CRO -> `@FrontendSystem + @TechnicalSEO` plus analytics/measurement specialist as relevant
- Outreach -> existing sales/outreach workflow and authorized mail tooling
- Pricing -> business/financial analysis lane under `@Orchestrator`
- MarketingIdeas/LeadMagnets/Copy -> existing social/brand/copy capabilities
- CompetitorIntel -> research + current evidence; no speculative competitor metrics

## Reviewed upstream
- `ognjengt/founder-skills` — MIT.
- Useful skill families include SOP creation, CRO, hooks, lead magnets, strategic planning, GTM, outreach, competitor intelligence, brand copy, pricing, PRDs and marketing ideas.
- Promotional claims such as "viral", "#1", "high-converting" or "Fortune 500 growth team" are not adopted as Ercan OS outcome promises.

## Context contract
Use a project/company context artifact when one exists. A `FOUNDER_CONTEXT.md`-style file may contain:
- company/product
- ICP/audience
- value proposition
- products/services
- brand voice
- channels
- business goals
- constraints
- current funnel/metrics
- decision horizon

Do not create or persist sensitive company/customer facts without a real need and user/project permission.

## Operating flow
`goal -> current constraint/bottleneck -> evidence -> options -> selected operating move -> owner -> artifact/action -> metric -> review trigger`

## Rules
1. Separate strategy from execution and execution from measurement.
2. Do not fabricate TAM, revenue, conversion, competitor or customer evidence.
3. Current market/platform facts require current sources when material.
4. Outreach uses existing anti-duplication, personalization, sender-identity and approval rules.
5. PRDs must include acceptance criteria, non-goals, dependencies, risks and verification.
6. SOPs must identify trigger, owner, inputs, steps, exception handling, evidence and completion.
7. Pricing recommendations must expose assumptions; never present guessed willingness-to-pay as fact.
8. CRO changes require measurable hypotheses and should preserve brand/accessibility/performance.
9. Do not fan out every founder skill; route only the smallest sufficient set.

## Completion evidence
Goal, evidence/assumptions, selected workstream, produced artifact/action, metric or acceptance criteria, unresolved dependencies and final state.
