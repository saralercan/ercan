# YouTube Growth Engine Operating Standard

This standard governs Ercan OS YouTube strategy, channel building, content production, growth analysis and monetization planning. It complements `BRAND_SOCIAL.md`, `AI_DISCOVERY_SEO.md` and the `youtube-growth-engine` skill.

## Principle

Treat YouTube as a content product and feedback system, not a seven-prompt trick. Strategy, production, packaging, publishing, analytics and monetization are separate capabilities connected by evidence.

The system must never present an income target, subscriber target or algorithm outcome as guaranteed.

## Operating lifecycle

`goal -> audience/problem -> niche evidence -> positioning -> channel identity -> 90-day programming -> episode research -> script -> packaging -> production QA -> publish -> analytics -> repurpose -> monetization test -> next iteration`

## Required workstreams

### Strategy
Define audience, jobs/problems, channel promise, content pillars, competitive alternatives, creator/brand advantage and monetization hypotheses.

### Programming
Maintain a sequenced content backlog. Balance:
- demand capture / search
- recommendation/browse-native topics
- authority/education
- proof/case-study
- conversion/offer
- experiments

Do not let every video serve every objective.

### Research and truth
Factual videos require source-backed research. Current/platform-specific claims use current authoritative sources. Never fabricate statistics, case studies, testimonials, sponsor interest, income, product results or audience quotes.

### Scripts
Optimize for comprehension, value density and forward motion. Hook quickly, satisfy the promise, use evidence/examples, avoid filler and close with a relevant next action or next video.

### Packaging
Titles and thumbnails are paired hypotheses. They must be materially distinct, readable at small size and truthful to the delivered content. Avoid misleading clickbait or fabricated outcomes.

### Discovery
Use topic/audience fit, packaging and content satisfaction as the core model. Metadata supports understanding and search eligibility but is not treated as a deterministic ranking formula.

### Publishing
Publishing is an authenticated external action. Generate publish-ready artifacts freely; mutate the real channel only through an authorized connection and preserve audit evidence.

### Analytics
Interpret performance by traffic source, channel maturity, topic and sample size. CTR alone is not success. Retention alone is not success. Use a multi-signal diagnosis connected to the business/channel objective.

### Monetization
Model multiple paths and their dependencies. Verify current platform eligibility before claiming ad or membership availability. Track unit economics and disclosures for sponsorship/affiliate/product routes.

### Repurposing
Repurpose validated ideas, not every asset. Adapt hook, length, caption, CTA and format to each destination platform.

## Decision records

For major channel decisions record:
- decision
- evidence
- assumptions
- alternatives
- expected signal
- review date
- outcome/learning

This prevents repeated strategy resets based on one viral or weak video.

## Quality gates

A material YouTube plan should pass:
1. audience/problem clarity
2. differentiation/brand fit
3. evidence-backed topic rationale
4. sequenced content architecture
5. production feasibility
6. truthful packaging
7. factual/policy review
8. publishing state accuracy
9. analytics measurement plan
10. monetization assumption/eligibility separation
11. cross-channel brand compliance

## Thread/prompt import rule

Public prompt threads may seed ideas but are discovery input only. Convert them into durable modules, remove unsupported guarantees and verify platform facts before adoption. Never copy an influencer's revenue claim into Ercan OS as a system promise.
